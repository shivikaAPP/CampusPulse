# CampusPlus design.md

Source of truth for everything the user sees: look, copy, pages, SEO text, legal pages and accessibility.
The frontend agent must read this file and `techstack.md` and `architecture.md` fully before writing code.
If a rule here is unclear, ask. Do not guess.

---

## 1. Hard bans (checked automatically and in review)

| Banned | What to do instead | How it is checked |
|---|---|---|
| Purple gradient, and any gradient at all | Solid colors only. Accent is cyan (section 4) | `lint:copy` fails on `gradient(` in CSS or TSX |
| Vague hero text | Hero names what the product lists and who it is for (section 9) | Review |
| Fake reviews and testimonials | No review section. No quotes from people | `lint:copy` fails on "testimonial"; review |
| Heavy scroll animation | No scroll-triggered animation of any kind | `lint:copy` fails on `IntersectionObserver` and `animation-timeline` |
| Pill-shaped buttons | Buttons use a 6px radius | `lint:copy` fails on radius of 100% or 3 digit px values and on `rounded-full` |
| Emoji as icons or anywhere in copy | Lucide SVG icons only | `lint:copy` fails on emoji characters |
| Fake metrics | Show only numbers computed from the real data on screen, such as "12 listings match" | Review; `lint:copy` fails on patterns like "10,000+ students" |
| Cursor animation | Default cursor only, `pointer` on clickable items | `lint:copy` fails on `cursor: url(` and `mousemove` listeners |
| AI generated images | No photos or illustrations. Use text, Lucide icons and the hand-built logo (section 8) | Review of `public/` and `src/assets/` |
| Em dashes | Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses | `lint:copy` fails on U+2014 |
| "Made with AI" tag or similar footer credit | No builder credit of any kind | `lint:copy` fails on "made with" and "generated by" |
| AI sounding copy | Plain, specific sentences (section 3) | `lint:copy` banned word list; review |

Also banned: lorem ipsum, placeholder names such as "John Doe", and any stock number, rating or count that is not computed from real data.

## 3. Voice and copy rules

Write like a careful senior student explaining something to a friend. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Say what a thing does.

Rules:
- No em dashes. No emoji. No exclamation marks in headings or buttons.
- Buttons start with a verb and name the object: "Save opportunity", "Open official page", "Mark as applied".
- Never say a student applied, registered or was selected unless they marked it themselves. Statuses are student managed.
- Never promise results. A high match score does not mean the student is eligible or will be selected.
- Use "Demo data" wherever a record is a sample.
- Numbers: always from data. Money is shown as `₹0`, shown as "Free" in badges, and `₹499` otherwise.
- Dates: `24 Nov 2026`, in Indian Standard Time, with weekday where useful. Never use "soon" without a date next to it.

Banned words and phrases (the lint script also checks these): seamless, seamlessly, unlock, elevate, supercharge, empower, empowers, empowering, revolutionize, revolutionise, cutting-edge, game-changing, leverage, delve, journey, tapestry, ecosystem, next-generation, unleash, "in today's fast-paced world".

Before and after examples:

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| Unlock your next big opportunity | Free hackathons, internships and workshops for college students |
| Your next opportunity is closer than you think | Search listings in one place, see how well each fits your profile, then track what you applied to |
| Supercharge your career | Check eligibility and finish the preparation checklist before the deadline |
| Join thousands of students | (omit, no real count exists) |

The blueprint tagline ("Discover More. Miss Nothing. Build Your Future.") is not used in the hero or in meta text. If the team wants it, use it only in the footer, once.

## 4. Design tokens

All tokens are CSS custom properties in `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`. Components must use tokens, never raw hex values.

### Color (dark theme, solid fills only)

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0B1020` | Page background |
| `--surface` | `#151C30` | Cards, inputs, header |
| `--surface-raised` | `#1C2540` | Hover state of cards, selected rows |
| `--border` | `#2A3555` | 1px borders |
| `--text` | `#F8FAFC` | Primary text |
| `--text-muted` | `#A6B0C3` | Secondary text |
| `--accent` | `#22D3EE` | Primary buttons, links, focus ring, active nav |
| `--accent-hover` | `#67E8F9` | Hover of accent items |
| `--on-accent` | `#0B1020` | Text on accent buttons |
| `--success` | `#34D399` | Verified, completed |
| `--warning` | `#FBBF24` | Closing soon |
| `--danger` | `#F87171` | Expired, errors, report |

Purple (`#8B5CF6`) from the blueprint is removed. There is a single accent color. No secondary accent.

Contrast: the pairs above are chosen to meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text). Verify each pair with a contrast checker before release and record the result in the PR description. Status is never shown by color alone: every status badge has an icon and a text label.

### Typography

- Font: Inter variable, self hosted through `@fontsource-variable/inter`, latin subset only. No Google Fonts request.
- Fallback stack: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
- `font-display: swap`. Preload the single woff2 file in `index.html`.
- Scale: 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 32, 40 px. Body is 16px with 1.6 line height. Headings 1.25 line height.
- H1: `clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)`, weight 700. H2: 1.5rem, weight 600. H3: 1.125rem, weight 600.
- Maximum text line length: 70 characters (`max-width: 70ch` on prose).

### Spacing, radius, elevation

- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 px.
- Page container: max width 1120px, 16px side gutter on mobile, 24px from 768px up.
- Radius: buttons and inputs 6px, cards 8px, badges 4px. No radius above 8px anywhere.
- No box shadows on cards. Use the 1px border. One exception: the modal overlay may use a flat `rgba(11, 16, 32, 0.7)` backdrop.

## 5. Components

### Buttons
- Primary: `--accent` background, `--on-accent` text, 6px radius, 40px high (44px on touch screens), 16px horizontal padding, weight 600.
- Secondary: transparent background, 1px `--border`, `--text` text.
- Destructive: transparent, 1px `--danger`, `--danger` text.
- Hover changes background color only (150ms). Active state is one shade darker. Disabled is 50% opacity with `aria-disabled` and a visible reason nearby.
- Visible focus ring on every interactive element: `outline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 2px`.

### Opportunity card
Order of content: category label with icon, title (H3 as a link to the detail page), organizer, fee badge, mode and city, deadline line, verification badge, match score block, "View details" and a bookmark icon button.
- Whole card is not a click target. Only the title link, "View details" and the bookmark button are.
- Demo records show a "Demo data" badge.
- Expired records are dimmed to 60% opacity, show "Closed", and hide the match score.

### Match score block
A number out of 100 with a label ("Strong", "Good", "Fair", "Low") and a horizontal bar made of a solid `--accent` fill on `--border`. No ring or gauge animation. Under the number, one sentence of explanation. When the profile is empty, show "Add your skills and interests to see match scores" with a link to the profile.

### Badges
4px radius, 12px text, icon plus label.
- Free: success outline. Paid: neutral outline with amount.
- Verified (check icon, success), Community reported (users icon, accent), Unverified (circle-help icon, muted), Expired (clock icon, danger), Demo data (flask-conical icon, dashed muted border), Closing soon (alarm-clock icon, warning).

### Form controls
Inputs 40px high, `--surface` background, 1px `--border`, 6px radius, visible label above (never placeholder only), inline error text under the field with `aria-describedby`.
Filter chips are rectangular toggle buttons (6px radius) with `aria-pressed`.

### Navigation
Header with the logo, links (Discover, Explore, Saved, Applications, Profile) and a search link on mobile. On mobile the nav collapses into a "Menu" button that opens a simple list. No hover mega menus. Active link has an accent underline and `aria-current="page"`.
Footer on every page: Terms, Privacy, contact email, and this sentence: "Listings come from third party sources. Check the official page before you apply."

### Icons
Lucide, one named import per icon (`import { Bookmark } from 'lucide-react'`). 16px or 20px, stroke width 1.75. Decorative icons get `aria-hidden="true"`. An icon that is the only content of a control gets an `aria-label`.

---

## 6. Motion

- Allowed: 150ms color and border transitions on hover and focus, and a 150ms opacity fade for the mobile menu and modal.
- Not allowed: scroll triggered effects, parallax, staggered entrance animations, counters that count up, marquee text, animated gradients, cursor effects, page transition effects, skeleton shimmer.
- Loading state is static skeleton blocks (flat `--surface` rectangles) or the text "Loading".
- `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` sets all transition durations to 0.

---

## 7. Imagery

No raster images in the first release. No AI generated images. No stock photos. Visual interest comes from typography, layout and Lucide icons.
Allowed assets: the logo SVG, favicon files, web manifest icons, and one Open Graph image (section 8) built from the logo and text in a design tool or from an SVG template, never generated by an image model.

## 9. Page specification

One `<h1>` per page. Every page has a unique `<title>`, unique meta description and unique `<h1>`. Titles are 60 characters or fewer where possible. Descriptions are 110 to 160 characters.

| Route | Title | Meta description | H1 | Indexable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | CampusPlus \| Free student hackathons, internships, workshops | Search hackathons, internships, workshops and competitions in one list. Filter by fee, city and deadline, check eligibility and track your applications. | Free hackathons, internships and workshops for college students | Yes |
| `/explore` | Explore student opportunities \| CampusPlus | Browse hackathons, internships, workshops, competitions and meetups. Combine filters for fee, mode, skill, level, city and deadline. | Explore opportunities | Yes |
| `/opportunities/:id` | `{title}` \| CampusPlus | Template: `{title} by {organizer}. {category}, {Free or ₹fee}, register by {deadline date}.` (trim to 160 characters) | The opportunity title | Yes if `isDemo` is false and the listing is not expired. Otherwise `noindex` |
| `/saved` | Saved opportunities \| CampusPlus | Your saved hackathons, internships and workshops on this device, with deadline labels and a link back to each listing. | Saved opportunities | No (`noindex`) |
| `/applications` | Application tracker \| CampusPlus | Track the status of each opportunity you applied to, update it yourself and finish the preparation checklist for every listing. | Application tracker | No (`noindex`) |
| `/profile` | Your profile \| CampusPlus | Set your skills, interests, budget and city so CampusPlus can calculate match scores. Your profile stays in this browser. | Your profile | No (`noindex`) |
| `/submit` | Submit an opportunity \| CampusPlus | Submit a hackathon, internship or workshop for review. Include the official source link so moderators can verify it. | Submit an opportunity | Yes |
| `/terms` | Terms and Conditions \| CampusPlus | The terms for using CampusPlus, including how listings, match scores and application tracking work and what we do not guarantee. | Terms and Conditions | Yes |
| `/privacy` | Privacy Policy \| CampusPlus | What data CampusPlus stores, where it is stored, how reports and submissions are handled and how to contact us about your data. | Privacy Policy | Yes |
| `/admin` | Admin \| CampusPlus | Moderation tools for CampusPlus listings, reports and submissions. | Moderation | No (`noindex`), disallowed in robots.txt |
| `*` (404) | Page not found \| CampusPlus | The page you requested does not exist. Return to the CampusPlus home page or browse all opportunities. | Page not found | No (`noindex`) |

Personal pages use `noindex` in a `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` tag and are not blocked in robots.txt. Blocking a page in robots.txt stops crawlers from reading its `noindex` tag.

### Home page layout (top to bottom)

1. Header and navigation.
2. Hero: the H1 from the table, one supporting sentence ("Browse listings in one place, see how well each one fits your profile, check eligibility and keep track of what you applied to."), the search field, and the city selector. No image.
3. Filter chips: All, Free only, Near you, Online, Offline, Closing soon.
4. "Recommended for you": up to 6 cards sorted by match score. If the profile is empty, show a short prompt with a link to `/profile` and sort by nearest deadline instead.
5. "Closing soon": non-expired listings with a deadline within 7 days, nearest first.
6. "Your shortlist today": 3 to 5 listings, each with a one line reason (rules in architecture.md).
7. "Recently added": the 4 newest listings by `createdAt`.
8. Footer.

Chip definitions: Online shows online and hybrid listings. Offline shows offline and hybrid listings. Near you shows offline and hybrid listings in the profile city, and asks for a city if none is set. Closing soon shows deadlines within 3 days. Free only shows `fee === 0`.

### Opportunity detail page
Sections in order: title and organizer, badges row, key facts (fee, additional costs, mode, city, event date, deadline), description, benefits, skills, eligibility result with reasons, match score with the per factor breakdown, verification block (status, source link, last checked date, "Report listing"), preparation checklist, actions (Save, Open official page, status selector).
- "Open official page" opens `applicationUrl` in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. If `applicationUrl` is null the button is replaced by the text "No official page is linked for this listing" (this is always the case for demo data).
- The page never says "registered" or "applied" on the student's behalf.
- Expired listings show a danger banner "Registration closed on {date}" and the apply action is removed.

### My applications page
Four columns on desktop and a stacked list on mobile: Interested, Applied, Shortlisted, Completed. Fixed explanatory sentence at the top: "You update these statuses yourself. CampusPlus does not receive updates from organizers or employers."

### Empty states
Every list has a specific empty state: what is empty, why, and one action. Example for Saved: "You have not saved any opportunities. Browse listings and use the bookmark button to keep them here." Explore with filters: "No listings match these filters." plus a Reset filters button. Verified only with zero results: "No verified listings yet. A listing is marked verified after a moderator checks its source."

## 10. Terms and Conditions page (`/terms`)

Plain language, numbered sections, a "Last updated" date that is set on the day of publishing, and a contact email from `VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL`. Content must cover:

1. Who we are and what CampusPlus does: a discovery and tracking tool, not an organizer, employer or application portal.
2. Third party listings: details come from organizers or community submissions. CampusPlus does not run these events or hire for these roles, and does not endorse them.
3. Verify before you act: students must confirm dates, fees and requirements on the official page. Fees shown are registration fees only. Travel, accommodation and equipment can cost extra.
4. Match score and eligibility checker: informational only, calculated from the data the student entered and the listing, with no guarantee of eligibility, selection or outcome.
5. Application tracking: statuses are entered by the student, are not confirmed by any organizer, and are stored in the student's browser.
6. Demo data: records labelled "Demo data" are samples and are not real opportunities.
7. Verification labels: what Verified, Community reported, Unverified and Expired mean.
8. Submissions and reports: what a user may submit, that submissions must be accurate and lawful, that moderators may edit or remove them, and that CampusPlus may decline any submission.
9. Acceptable use: no scraping that breaks the law or these terms, no spam submissions, no attempts to disrupt the service.
10. Intellectual property: the CampusPlus name, logo and software belong to the project owners; listing content belongs to its sources.
11. Availability and changes: the service is provided as is and can change or stop. Terms can be updated and the date at the top changes.
12. Limitation of liability: to the extent permitted by law, CampusPlus is not liable for missed deadlines, rejected applications, incorrect listings or losses from third party events.
13. Governing law: laws of India. Court location to be filled in by the owners.
14. Contact.

The owners must fill in the legal entity or individual name, the contact email and the jurisdiction. These must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before the site is shown as a real service. The agent must not invent a company name, address or registration number.

## 11. Privacy Policy page (`/privacy`)

Plain language, "Last updated" date, contact email. Content must cover:

1. Summary at the top: no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies at launch.
2. Data stored in your browser only: profile (display name, college, city, year, skills, interests, level, mode, budget), saved listings, application statuses and checklist progress. This data stays on the device and is not sent to our servers. Clearing browser data removes it. Provide a "Delete my data on this device" button on `/profile` that clears all `cp.v1.*` keys.
3. Data sent to our servers: reports about a listing (listing id, reason, optional note, optional email if the user provides it), opportunity submissions (listing details and optional email), and standard request data (IP address and user agent) handled by the hosting provider and used for rate limiting and security. The API does not store IP addresses in its database.
4. Admin session cookie: used only on `/admin`, httpOnly, for moderators. No cookies for ordinary visitors.
5. Why we use the data: to show relevant listings, review reports and submissions, and keep the service secure.
6. Sharing: no sale of data. Hosting and infrastructure providers process requests on our behalf. List the actual providers once chosen.
7. Retention: reports and submissions are kept until reviewed and for a stated period afterward (owners to set, for example 12 months), then deleted.
8. Analytics: none at launch. If analytics is added later, this page must be updated first and the analytics must be cookie free or consent based.
9. Your rights under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, such as access, correction and erasure, and how to ask by email. Name a grievance contact.
10. Children: the service is aimed at college students. Do not target under 18 users.
11. External links: official pages are third party sites with their own policies.
12. Changes and contact.

The owners must review this text against what the final build actually collects. If the build collects anything not listed here, the policy changes before release.

## 12. 404 page

- Shown for any unknown route and for an unknown opportunity id (with the message "This listing does not exist or has been removed").
- Content: H1 "Page not found", one sentence, and two buttons: "Go to home page" and "Browse all opportunities". Same header and footer as the rest of the site.
- Sets `noindex`.
- Because a single page app host serves `index.html` with status 200 for unknown paths, this is a soft 404. The `noindex` tag is the mitigation. Record this as a known limit.

## 13. Accessibility and responsiveness

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- `lang="en"` on `html`. Skip link "Skip to main content" as the first focusable element.
- Landmarks: `header`, `nav`, `main`, `footer`. One `main` per page.
- All interactive elements work with the keyboard. Modals trap focus, close on Escape and return focus to the opener.
- Touch targets at least 44 by 44 px on mobile.
- Test at widths 360, 768 and 1280 px. No horizontal page scroll at 360px.
- Forms: every field has a label, errors are announced and linked with `aria-describedby`.
- Text can be zoomed to 200% without loss of content.
